Companion artifacts for Hyra: Hunyuan Research Agent.

This repository collects concrete solutions that Hyra produced across a range of open problems in science, mathematics, engineering, and creative design, released alongside the Hyra launch post. Each folder holds the final solution artifact and, where relevant, the self-contained scripts that reproduce it.

📝 Launch post: hy.tencent.com/research/hyra

🚀 Hyra 2.0 blog: hy.tencent.ai/research/hyra2

  • 2026-11-04: 🌊 HyPDE, a 2D foundation model for PDEs, released with weights and code. HyPDE, a 323M-parameter fused-history transformer trained from scratch on thirteen 2D physics datasets, forecasts simulated fields from their last six frames. Zero-shot on the test split of thirteen datasets of The Well and FlowBench, against Walrus (1.29B parameters, released weights, no fine-tuning on either side), it has the lower median VRMSE on 9 of 13 datasets in one-step forecasting, 10 of 13 over rollout frames 1-20 and 8 of 13 over frames 21-60. Weights, the hypde package and the full table: AI4Science/hypde/.

  • 2026-11-03: 🔐 Certified distances of non-CSS quantum codes. The qldpc-challenge board labels all 184 of its general (non-CSS) stabilizer codes as upper bounds only. hyqec, a prover plus an independently written certificate checker, proves the exact Pauli-weight distance of 165 of them: 122 with SAT proofs checked by drat-trim, 42 with re-executable branch-and-bound records, and the board's [[452,115,18]] (d = 18) with a coverage certificate of 124.6 billion search nodes that the independent checker re-executed in full. Code: AI4Science/hyqec/.

  • 2026-11-02: 🚀 Hyra 2.0 blog released. Read Hyra 2.0: The Next Step for Auto Research for an overview of Hyra's progress in developing reusable scientific tools, models, and workflows.

  • 2026-11-02: 🧫 HyCell, a single-cell embedding model, released with weights and code. HyCell, a 16.1M-parameter model, maps the raw counts of a human cell to a 512-dimensional embedding and decodes embeddings back to gene expression. On seven atlases held out from pretraining, its frozen embeddings reach an overall score of 0.735, level with Stack (0.736, 218M parameters). Weights, the hycell package and the technical report: AI4Science/hycell/.

  • 2026-11-02: ⚛️ HyFF force fields released with weights and code. Five machine-learning force fields for molecular simulation, from 2.3M to 1.2B parameters, covering 83 elements with total charge and spin and trained on OMol25. On the full OMol25 validation set, HyFF Andante reaches a force error comparable to UMA-S-1.2 (7.65 vs 7.55 meV/Å) at 29× its MD speed, and HyFF Largo has the lowest energy error of the evaluated models (0.364 meV/atom; DPA4-Pro: 0.380) at 9.7× DPA4-Pro's speed (MD of a 2,586-atom system on one H20). The hyff package runs NVE, NVT and NPT molecular dynamics and batched prediction: AI4Science/hyff/.

  • 2026-11-02: 🧬 Two protein models released with weights and code. HyInv, a 1.96M-parameter inverse-folding model, and HyPLM-Flash, a 650M-parameter protein language model with a fast INT8 inference engine, are published under AI4Science/ together with their weights, inference code and technical reports: AI4Science/hyinv/, AI4Science/hyplm_flash/.

  • 2026-11-02: 📐 Two results on Petty's and Yau's conjectures.

    • Petty's equilateral-set conjecture in dimension five. The highest settled dimension rises from 4 (Makeev, 2005) to 5: every five-dimensional real normed space contains six equidistant points. Paper: AI4Science/petty_dimension_five/.
    • Yau's circle-action conjecture (unrestricted form). A closed seven-manifold with strictly positive sectional curvature admits no effective smooth circle action, giving a counterexample. The manifold is not simply connected; the simply connected version remains open. Paper: AI4Science/yau_circle_action_conjecture/.

  • 2026-08-19: 🧾 Three new results, each with a Lean 4 formalization of its finite and numerical core.

    • Beurling–Ahlfors transform. Iwaniec's 1982 conjecture predicts the sharp Lᵖ operator norm p*−1; the best proven uniform coefficient drops from 1.575 (Bañuelos and Janakiraman, 2008) to 1.523958. Paper: AI4Science/beurling_ahlfors_bellman/.
    • Partial Hadamard matrices. Their asymptotic count is now known throughout every power-law range above n², not just the cubic range m ≫ n³, lowering the critical exponent from 3 (arXiv:2603.30013) to 2. Paper: AI4Science/partial_hadamard_counting/.
    • Commutators close to the identity. The identity is never a commutator, but comes within ε at a price: the least ‖D‖·‖X‖ needed falls from Tao's O(log⁵(1/ε)), since refined to O(log⁴), to O(log³(1/ε)), against Popa's log(1/ε) lower bound. Paper: AI4Science/commutator_log_cubed/.

  • 2026-08-17: 📐 Blaschke–Lebesgue in three dimensions. The least volume of a convex body of constant width w is conjectured to be Meissner's ≈ 0.419860 w³; the best certified lower bound rises from 4π/33 · w³ ≈ 0.380799 w³ (Nishioka, arXiv:2606.01754) to > 0.411040 w³, closing 77.4% of the remaining gap. Paper: AI4Science/3d_blaschke_lebesgue/.

  • 2026-07-29: 🎉 The sum-vs-difference problem is settled. For finite A ⊆ ℤ, the optimal exponent relating |A+A| to |A−A| has supremum exactly 2, approached arbitrarily closely but never attained: a complete, machine-checked resolution going beyond the sums_diffs record below. Proof: sum-diff-proof.

  • m S.-T. Yau: Open problems in geometry, Proc. Sympos. Pure Math. 54, Part 1 (1993), 1–28; see also Structure of manifolds with positive curvature based on geometric analysis, Notices of the ICCM 1(2) (2013), 24–27, for the smooth circle-action conjecture.
  • n The qldpc-challenge board (github.com/unitaryfoundation/qldpc-challenge) labels every one of its general stabilizer (non-CSS) entries as an upper bound (d≤) only, as its certifier does not minimise Pauli weight (TRACKS.md, 2026-10-27). Metrics are as defined by each benchmark; comparisons are against the cited published results and conjecture statements.

Metric notes.

  • C₁: first autoconvolution/autocorrelation constant, max(f∗f)/(∫f)² (minimize).
  • R: second autocorrelation ratio, ‖f∗f‖₂² / (‖f∗f‖₁·‖f∗f‖∞) (maximize).
  • C₅: Erdős minimum-overlap constant (minimize).
  • C(A): sum-vs-difference exponent, log(|A+A|/|A|) / log(|A−A|/|A|) (maximize).
  • params: unique trainable parameters of a transformer that adds two 10-digit integers at ≥ 0.99 accuracy (minimize).
  • objective: PARP1 docking objective, Vina score + 10·(1 − QED) (minimize).
  • CNOTs added: extra CNOTs inserted by SWAP routing (minimize; 1 SWAP = 3 CNOTs).
  • forecast R²: rolling-origin, free-running (24-month) forecast R² for monthly sunspot numbers (maximize).
  • Vol/w³ bound: largest proven universal lower bound on Vol(K)/w³ over all convex bodies K ⊂ ℝ³ of constant width w (maximize; the conjectured optimum is ≈ 0.419860).
  • C_BA: smallest proven uniform coefficient in ‖B‖_{Lᵖ→Lᵖ} ≤ C_BA·(p*−1) for the Beurling–Ahlfors transform, over all 1 < p < ∞ (minimize; the conjectured optimum is 1).
  • regime exponent: infimum of the power-law exponents u for which m/nᵘ → ∞ is known to force the asymptotic formula for N(n,m) (minimize; the endpoint value itself is not claimed).
  • log exponent: smallest proven k in m(ε) = O(logᵏ(1/ε)) for commutators within ε of the identity (minimize; the matching lower bound is k = 1).
  • dimension settled: highest dimension n for which every real n-dimensional normed space is known to contain n + 1 equidistant points, as conjectured by Petty (maximize).
  • conjecture status: status of the unrestricted claim that every closed manifold admitting strictly positive sectional curvature admits an effective smooth circle action. The counterexample has π₁ ≅ (ℤ/6)²; the simply connected version remains open.
  • board non-CSS codes with exact d: number of the board's 184 general stabilizer codes whose Pauli-weight distance is proven exactly, not only bounded (maximize).

Trained models, each with its weights, inference code and technical report:

  • AI4Science/hyinv/: HyInv, a 1.96-million-parameter message-passing graph network for protein inverse folding (backbone → sequence), trained with structure-reward reinforcement learning. The 7.8 MB weights ship in the folder; see its README for the CLI and Python API.
  • AI4Science/hyplm_flash/: HyPLM-Flash, a 650-million-parameter protein language model with an INT8 GPU inference engine (about 130,000 residues per second on one H20). The 2.6 GB weights are a GitHub Release on this repository; weights/download.sh fetches, merges and verifies them.
  • AI4Science/hycell/: HyCell, a 16.1-million-parameter embedding model for single-cell transcriptomes, pretrained without cell-type labels on 79.5 million human cells of the CZ CELLxGENE Census. It maps raw counts to 512-dimensional embeddings and decodes them back to expression, and can be fine-tuned into a cell-type annotator. The 64 MB weights ship in the folder as part of the hycell package.
  • AI4Science/hyff/: HyFF, five machine-learning force fields for molecular simulation (Presto, Vivace, Moderato, Andante and Largo; 2.3 million to 1.2 billion parameters, 83 elements), with the hyff package for single-structure inference, NVE/NVT/NPT molecular dynamics and batched prediction. The weights (9 MB to 4.8 GB per model) are a GitHub Release on this repository; the package downloads and verifies them on first use.

Creative and game-playing demos (no leaderboard comparison):

  • AI4Fun/music/: a five-part arrangement of 望春風 (Bāng-chhun-hong, 1933, 鄧雨賢).
  • AI4Fun/3d_penguin/: a procedural 3-D QQ-penguin built by a single Blender bpy script.
  • AI4Fun/3d_hunyuan/: a procedural 3-D Tencent Hunyuan (混元) logo orb.
