The provision, which goes into effect in 90 days, will “undermine” the ability of companies in those countries from using U.S. chips to train advanced A.I. models, the administration said in a news release.

政府在新闻稿中表示，该条款将于90天后生效，将“削弱”这些国家的企业使用美国芯片训练高级人工智能模型的能力。

The plan to crack down on China’s ability to access the coveted A.I. chips comes as President Trump is scheduled to travel to Asia. He will travel to the Philippines and then to China for an economic summit, where he is expected to meet with the Chinese leader, Xi Jinping and discuss the war in Iran, nuclear nonproliferation and trade. The data center rules could create a new source of friction between the countries, at a time when Mr. Trump has been trying to keep a fragile trade truce with China intact.

这项旨在遏制中国获取备受追捧的人工智能芯片能力的计划出台之际，特朗普总统正计划前往亚洲。他将先访问菲律宾，然后前往中国参加一场经济峰会。预计他将在那里与中国领导人习近平会面，讨论伊朗战争、核不扩散以及贸易问题。在特朗普一直试图维持与中国脆弱的贸易休战之际，数据中心的规定可能会成为两国之间新的摩擦源。

Share prices of chip stocks tumbled Monday. Nvidia fell more than 4 percent. Intel fell more than 7 percent, and Micron nearly 8 percent. Indexes that track these stocks fell too — the Philadelphia Semiconductor index sank more than 5 percent, and the broader, tech-heavy Nasdaq slumped, too.

芯片类股票的价格在周一暴跌。英伟达跌幅超过4%。英特尔跌幅超过7%，美光跌幅近8%。追踪这些股票的指数也出现了下跌——费城半导体指数下跌超过5%，范围更广、以科技股为主的纳斯达克指数也出现了大幅下挫。

The Trump administration said on Monday that it was moving forward with a new rule that would clamp down on China’s ability to access advanced A.I. chips through the use of data centers outside China’s borders.

特朗普政府周一表示，正在推进一项新规定，旨在限制中国通过利用境外数据中心获取先进人工智能芯片的能力。

The rule aims to close what many in Washington see as a loophole allowing China to sidestep a ban that prevents Chinese firms from buying the most advanced A.I. chips made by companies like Nvidia. Chinese firms have continued to legally access the chips by renting them from data centers in Southeast Asia and elsewhere. Whether to close the loophole has been a significant source of contention within the Trump administration.

华盛顿许多人认为，中国利用一个漏洞规避了禁止中国企业购买由英伟达等公司制造的最先进人工智能芯片的禁令，这项规定就旨在堵住这一漏洞。中国企业一直通过从东南亚及其他地区的数据中心租用这些芯片，继续合法地获取它们。是否要堵住这个漏洞，一直是特朗普政府内部争论的重要源头。

The new rule is intended to make it harder for Chinese firms to remotely access the chips. It will require overseas data centers to obtain a government license before supplying “significant volumes of computing power” to firms with headquarters in a small group of high-risk countries, a list that includes China, Russia and Iran.

新规旨在使中国企业更难远程访问这些芯片。它将要求海外数据中心在向总部位于少数高风险国家（包括中国、俄罗斯和伊朗）的企业提供“大量计算能力”之前，必须获得政府许可。

Data centers are also required to perform strict checks on their customers to determine if they are linked to those countries. And the U.S. will now require a license to export advanced chips to companies with headquarters in Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand or Brazil, which have a higher than normal risk of smuggling, the administration said.

数据中心还被要求对其客户进行严格审查，以确定他们是否与这些国家有关联。此外，美国政府表示，美国现在将要求向总部位于印度尼西亚、马来西亚、新加坡、泰国或巴西的公司出口先进芯片时获得许可，因为这些国家走私的风险高于正常水平。

The formerly obscure issue of chip sales to foreign data centers was thrust into the spotlight in recent weeks with the revelations about Megaspeed, a Singaporean data center firm created by a woman who had an affair with Jensen Huang, who was ousted as Nvidia’s chief executive last week. U.S. authorities deemed the woman, Alice Huang, to be an asset for Chinese intelligence services, and they are investigating Megaspeed over allegations of smuggling chips to China. (Chinese officials have denied that Ms. Huang is their spy.)

最近几周，随着关于Megaspeed的内幕曝光，原本鲜为人知的向外国数据中心销售芯片的问题被推到了聚光灯下。Megaspeed是一家新加坡数据中心公司，由一名曾与黄仁勋有过婚外情的女性创立，而黄仁勋上周刚被罢免了英伟达首席执行官的职务。美国当局认为这名名叫黄乐(Alice Huang)的女性是中国情报部门的线人，目前他们正就Megaspeed涉嫌向中国走私芯片一事进行调查。（中国官员否认黄乐是他们的间谍。）

But some officials in the Trump administration and in Congress increasingly appear to believe that Chinese access to these data centers has undercut U.S. export controls and allowed China’s artificial intelligence ecosystem to flourish. Some worry this could give Beijing an economic edge, as well as a military one.

但特朗普政府和国会的一些官员似乎越来越认为，中国能够访问这些数据中心削弱了美国的出口管制，让中国的人工智能生态系统得以蓬勃发展。一些人担心这可能会给北京带来经济和军事上的优势。

That scandal has turned China’s access to advanced A.I. computing power into a subject of debate at the highest levels of the administration, with the Commerce Department, which oversees the regulations, clashing over them with other parts of the government.

这桩丑闻使中国获取先进人工智能计算能力的问题成为政府最高层辩论的话题，负责监督这些规定的商务部在这一问题上与政府其他部门发生了冲突。

The debate has set Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick, who oversees chip policy, at odds with Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent, Secretary of State Marco Rubio and the U.S. trade representative, Jamieson Greer, who have all pushed to reinstate broad restrictions on data centers in recent months.

这场辩论使得负责芯片政策的商务部长霍华德·卢特尼克与财政部长斯科特·贝森特、国务卿马可·鲁比奥以及美国贸易代表贾米森·格里尔产生了分歧，后者在最近几个月里一直都在推动恢复对数据中心的广泛限制。

The announcement on Monday said that the new rule was “written by and at the direction of the Department of Treasury and the National Security Council” — both agencies that have pushed for more stringent rules.

周一的声明称，新规是“在财政部和国家安全委员会的指示下编写的”——这两个机构都曾推动制定更严格的规定。

Chris McGuire, a former Biden administration official who is now a senior fellow at the Council on Foreign Relations, said that the line was “completely unprecedented.”

前拜登政府官员、现任美国外交关系委员会高级研究员克里斯·麦奎尔表示，这句话“完全是史无前例的”。

“That implies that Commerce was dragged kicking and screaming over the finish line to publish the rule that the White House wanted,” he said.

“这意味着商务部是在强力施压下，不情不愿地被迫发布了白宫想要的那项规定，”他说。

Those tensions spilled out into the open on Friday, when Mr. Bessent blamed Mr. Lutnick’s department in a TV interview for failing to sanction Megaspeed, saying that other departments had pushed for the measure but that the Commerce Department “has the pen.” Mr. Bessent added that Nvidia should be “held accountable” if it did not do enough to prevent illegal diversion of chips to China.

这种紧张局势在周五公开化。当时贝森特在一次电视采访中指责卢特尼克的部门未能制裁Megaspeed，他表示其他部门曾推动这一措施，但商务部“掌握着决定权”。贝森特还说，如果英伟达在防止芯片被非法转移到中国方面做得不够，就应该“被追究责任”。

Both Mr. Bessent and Mr. Lutnick are traveling alongside Mr. Trump this week as he visits the Philippines and meets with Mr. Xi in China.

贝森特和卢特尼克本周都陪同特朗普出访，特朗普将访问菲律宾并在中国与习近平会面。

When asked about Mr. Bessent’s comments on Monday, Mr. Trump said there were just “two different views.”

当周一被问及贝森特的言论时，特朗普表示，这只是“两种不同的观点”。

“One view is, you don’t want to insult China,” the president said, adding: “The other view is, just go down and dirty and do what you have to do. You can’t say either is wrong or right.”

“一种观点是，你不想侮辱中国，”总统说。“另一种观点是，直接下手，做你必须做的事。你不能说哪一个是错的或是对的。”

The president said that Mr. Lutnick might have delayed actions against Megaspeed, but that he was doing it because “if you didn’t delay it, if you didn’t do something, you could have a blowup with China.”

总统表示，卢特尼克可能推迟了针对Megaspeed的行动，但他之所以这么做，是因为“如果你不推迟，如果你不采取一些措施，你可能会与中国产生冲突。”

“I don’t think you want to insult China just before you get there,” he said.

Firms like Megaspeed have legally been able to operate data centers in Southeast Asia that remotely serve Chinese clients. That means Chinese companies that are blocked from buying advanced Nvidia chips directly can simply rent them through Southeast Asian firms.

像Megaspeed这样的公司一直能够在东南亚合法运营数据中心，为中国客户提供远程服务。这意味着那些被禁止直接购买先进英伟达芯片的中国公司只需通过东南亚公司租用这些芯片即可。

Nvidia and its proponents contend that the sales help create jobs in the United States and ensure that companies around the world are reliant on American technology.

英伟达及其支持者认为，这些销售有助于在美国创造就业机会，并确保世界各地的公司依赖美国技术。
