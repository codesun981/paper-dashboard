Mr. Trump viewed Nvidia’s fortunes as bound up with those of the U.S. economy and the stock market. When reports surfaced that the company’s board was considering removing Mr. Huang, Mr. Trump asked confidants what they thought would happen to the markets. He was worried that Mr. Huang’s ouster could cause a sharp downturn.

特朗普认为英伟达的命运与美国经济和股市紧密相连。当有报道称该公司董事会正考虑罢免黄仁勋时，特朗普曾询问亲信，他们认为市场会作何反应。他担心罢免黄仁勋可能引发市场急剧下跌。

The president’s last-ditch effort to save Mr. Huang was a remarkable moment in a relationship that barely existed two years ago.

这是总统为挽救黄仁勋所做的最后努力，这在两人这段两年前还几乎不存在的关系中是一个引人注目的时刻。

During Mr. Trump’s first term, Mr. Huang was nowhere on his radar. The Nvidia founder had no real presence in Washington. He did not lobby or give to campaigns, and showed little interest in politics. The Biden administration’s sweeping restrictions on chip exports forced Mr. Huang to be more attentive to Washington, but he still did far less politicking than his rivals. In contrast to other tech leaders, he did not attend Mr. Trump’s second inauguration.

在特朗普的第一任期内，黄仁勋完全不在他的关注范围内。这位英伟达创始人在华盛顿几乎没有任何存在感。他不进行游说，也不向竞选活动捐款，对政治几乎不感兴趣。拜登政府对芯片出口实施的全面限制迫使黄仁勋更加关注华盛顿，但他在政治上的运作仍远少于竞争对手。与其他科技界领袖不同，他没有出席特朗普的第二次就职典礼。

With evident relish, Mr. Trump has told the story of first hearing Mr. Huang’s name. “Who the hell is he? What’s his name?” he has recalled to associates. “His name is Jensen Huang of Nvidia. I said, ‘What the hell is Nvidia?’”

特朗普曾兴致勃勃地向身边人讲起自己第一次听到黄仁勋名字时的情景。“他到底是谁？叫什么名字？”他回忆道。“他英伟达的黄仁勋。我说：‘英伟达到底是什么？’”

By late 2024, Mr. Trump had learned that Mr. Huang was the indispensable man at the company that controlled at least 80 percent of the market for chips used to train the most advanced A.I. models. And Mr. Huang saw an opening to woo a president who demanded personal attention and responded to flattery.

到了2024年底，特朗普了解到，在控制着至少80%最先进人工智能模型训练芯片市场的这家公司里，黄仁勋是不可或缺的人物。而黄仁勋也看到了一个契机，能够靠近这位渴望个人关注和恭维的总统。

President Trump called Jensen Huang on Tuesday, before the Nvidia chief stepped down amid a rapidly unfolding spy scandal, and urged him to keep fighting for his job, according to two people briefed on the conversation.

据两名了解此次通话的人士透露，在英伟达首席执行官于迅速发酵的间谍丑闻中辞职之前，特朗普总统周二曾致电黄仁勋，敦促他继续为保住职位而抗争。

The call was the latest instance of Mr. Trump defending and protecting Mr. Huang, who had become a high-profile ally and an influential adviser on artificial intelligence. The president continued to back him to the end, even after U.S. intelligence agencies had concluded that Mr. Huang was having an affair with a woman believed to be a Chinese spy.

此次通话是特朗普为黄仁勋辩护和提供庇护的最新例证。黄仁勋已成为他的一位备受瞩目的盟友，也是人工智能领域颇具影响力的顾问。即使在美国情报机构认定黄仁勋与一名被认为是中国间谍的女子有婚外情之后，总统仍一直支持他到最后。

Nvidia announced on Thursday that Mr. Huang, who built the company into the world’s leading supplier of advanced A.I. chips, had been replaced as chief executive following revelations not just of the affair with the woman suspected of being a spy, but also another with one of his employees.

英伟达周四宣布，在被曝出不仅与那名涉嫌为间谍的女子有婚外情，还与一位下属存在另一段婚外情之后，将公司打造成全球领先先进人工智能芯片供应商的黄仁勋已不再担任首席执行官一职。

A representative for Mr. Huang did not provide a comment for this article. Neither Mr. Trump nor the White House responded to requests for comment about his call to Mr. Huang.

黄仁勋的一名代表未就本文发表评论。特朗普和白宫均未回应就他致电黄仁勋一事提出的置评请求。

The president had been warned at least twice about Mr. Huang’s relationship with a Chinese citizen known as Alice Huang, whom intelligence agencies concluded had been recruited by China’s Ministry of State Security, The New York Times has reported. The first warning was late last year, and the second was in the spring.

据《纽约时报》此前报道，总统曾至少两次收到有关黄仁勋与一名被称为黄乐(Alice Huang)的中国公民之间关系的警告，情报机构认定该女子已被中国国家安全部招募。首次警告是在去年年底，第二次是在春季。

Mr. Trump took no action either time, and brought Mr. Huang with him on a trip to China in May. In recent days, he has publicly praised Mr. Huang and expressed sympathy for him, saying in a social media post on Thursday that he was a “legend.”

特朗普两次均未采取任何行动，并在5月访华时带上了黄仁勋。近几天来，他公开称赞黄仁勋并对其表示同情，周四还在社交媒体发帖称他是一位“传奇人物”。

The president’s advisers said he was blasé about the national security implications when told of the intelligence community assessments related to Mr. Huang. One aide said that Mr. Trump saw the episode mainly through the lens of adultery, and that the president dismissed the idea of Mr. Huang’s entanglement posing a threat to the country.

总统的顾问们表示，当被告知情报界对黄仁勋的相关评估时，特朗普对其中的国家安全影响不以为意。一名幕僚称，特朗普主要从婚外情的角度看待这一事件，并驳斥了黄仁勋的这段纠葛对国家构成威胁的说法。

Mr. Trump saw Nvidia as essentially too big to fail, aides said. The company, one of the world’s largest by market capitalization, has played a vital role in the global economy and underpinned the president’s hopes for A.I. dominance over China.

幕僚们表示，特朗普本质上认为英伟达“大到不能倒”。作为全球市值最高的公司之一，英伟达在全球经济中发挥着至关重要的作用，并支撑着总统在人工智能领域压制中国的希望。

Advisers to Mr. Trump gave several explanations for the way he had handled the matter, speaking on the condition of anonymity to describe sensitive conversations.

特朗普的顾问们在要求匿名的前提下描述了相关敏感对话，对他处理此事的方式给出了几点解释。

Chief among them was that the president had invested greatly in Mr. Huang, whom he saw as a modern-day Henry Ford, leading in a technology that could define the century.

其中最主要的一点是，总统在黄仁勋身上倾注了大量精力，将他视为当代亨利·福特，认为他正引领着一项可能定义本世纪的技术。

In April 2025, Mr. Huang and his wife, Lori, attended a dinner at Mar-a-Lago with Mr. Trump and a group of tech and crypto donors. The president came away intrigued, and the more he learned, the more impressed he became, people who spoke with him at the time said. He was told again and again that the A.I. boom ran almost entirely through Nvidia. “You’re taking over the world, Jensen,” Mr. Trump would later tell him.

2025年4月，黄仁勋和妻子洛丽在马阿拉歌出席了特朗普与一批科技及加密货币领域捐赠者的晚宴。当时与总统交谈过的人士表示，特朗普在晚宴后对黄仁勋产生了浓厚兴趣，而且了解得越多，印象就越深刻。他一次又一次地被告知，人工智能热潮几乎完全依托英伟达运转。“你正在接管世界，詹森，”特朗普后来对他说。

Mr. Huang offered something the president had failed to get in his first term and hoped to achieve in his second. During his first administration, Mr. Trump had pledged to bring back coal and steel jobs. But those industries never returned to their old strength.

黄仁勋提供了特朗普在第一任期内未能获得、并希望在第二任期内实现的东西。在第一任期内，特朗普曾承诺让煤炭和钢铁行业的工作岗位回流。但这些行业从未恢复往日的实力。

In a second term, A.I. offered another route to the reindustrialization of the United States that Mr. Trump had promised voters. He came to believe that the rush to build data centers, and their enormous appetite for electricity, could produce a manufacturing revival and keep an otherwise shaky economy running hot, people close to him said. To the president, that made the leaders of the A.I. companies the most valuable executives in the country, and Mr. Huang perhaps the most valuable of them all.

在第二任期内，人工智能为特朗普向选民承诺的美国再工业化提供了另一条路径。接近特朗普的人士表示，他逐渐相信，建设数据中心的热潮及其对电力的庞大需求能够带来制造业复兴，并让原本摇摇欲坠的经济保持强劲势头。在总统看来，这使得人工智能公司的领导者成为全国最具价值的高管，而黄仁勋或许是其中最具价值的一位。

Mr. Huang played into that savior role. In 2025, he said Nvidia would produce up to $500 billion of A.I. infrastructure in the United States. He also praised the president lavishly.

黄仁勋顺势扮演起了这一救世主角色。2025年，他表示英伟达将在美国生产价值高达5000亿美元的人工智能基础设施。他还对总统大加赞赏。

“America’s unique advantage that no country could possibly have is President Trump,” he said that July. He traveled with Mr. Trump to the Persian Gulf in May 2025, where the president announced deals for Saudi Arabia and the United Arab Emirates to buy Nvidia chips. He provided the kind of deal-making headlines that Mr. Trump craved.

“美国所拥有的、任何其他国家都不可能具备的独特优势，就是特朗普总统，”他在当年7月说道。2025年5月，他曾随特朗普前往波斯湾，期间总统宣布了沙特阿拉伯和阿拉伯联合酋长国购买英伟达芯片的协议。他提供了特朗普所渴望的那种达成交易的热点新闻。

Mr. Huang had persuaded Mr. Trump that Nvidia should sell its chips as widely as possible, so that the world would build on U.S., rather than Chinese, technology. David Sacks, the White House’s A.I. and crypto czar, backed him. The two men helped persuade Mr. Trump to plant himself on the unpopular side of a major issue for the populist right.

黄仁勋曾说服特朗普，英伟达应当尽可能广泛地销售芯片，以便世界建立在美国而非中国的技术之上。白宫人工智能与加密货币事务主管戴维·萨克斯支持他的这一立场。这两人在一个重大议题上合力说服特朗普站到了不受民粹主义右翼欢迎的一边。

China hawks on the MAGA right, including Stephen K. Bannon, wanted chip sales to China banned outright. But Mr. Trump sided with Mr. Huang and his billionaire counterparts leading other tech companies, including OpenAI, Meta and SpaceX.

包括斯蒂芬·K·班农在内的“让美国再次伟大”(MAGA)右翼对华鹰派人士希望全面禁止向中国出售芯片。但特朗普站在了黄仁勋以及领导OpenAI、Meta和SpaceX等其他科技公司的亿万富豪同行一边。

At Mr. Huang’s urging, Mr. Trump allowed Nvidia to sell certain models of its chips to China under an unusual arrangement in which the federal government took 15 percent, and later 25 percent, of the revenue. Critics raised legal questions and warned that national security was being sold out. Administration officials privately marveled at how much sway Mr. Huang seemed to have with the president.

在黄仁勋的敦促下，特朗普允许英伟达根据一项不同寻常的安排向中国出售特定型号的芯片，根据该安排，联邦政府抽取了相关营收的15%，后来提高到25%。批评人士提出了法律质疑，并警告称国家安全正在被出卖。政府官员私下里对黄仁勋似乎在总统面前拥有如此大的影响力感到惊叹。

Some U.S. officials had worried about Mr. Huang long before the spy scandal broke into the open this fall. They saw him as, at best, naïve and careless in his dealings with Chinese executives and local officials, with whom he kept close ties. Some wondered how he could possibly have been oblivious to Ms. Huang’s possible connections to Chinese intelligence.

早在今年秋季这起间谍丑闻公开曝光之前，一些美国官员就已经对黄仁勋感到担忧。在他们看来，黄仁勋在与他保持密切联系的中国企业高管和地方官员打交道时，往好的方面说也是天真且粗心大意。一些人纳闷，他怎么可能对黄乐与中国情报部门可能存在的联系毫无察觉。

But Mr. Trump was never likely to share in those national security concerns. While he has used bellicose campaign rhetoric against China — he has accused Beijing of “raping” the American economy and made that line of attack a focus of his 2016 presidential campaign — he has never been a China hawk in any conventional sense.

但特朗普从来就不太可能认同这些国家安全方面的担忧。尽管他在竞选中表现出好战的对华姿态——他曾指责北京“强奸”美国经济，并将这一攻击论调作为2016年总统竞选的重点——但他从来都不是传统意义上的对华鹰派。

Throughout the president’s terms, his aggressive posture on China has been limited to trade. And even there he has repeatedly shown he would rather cut a deal with Xi Jinping, the country’s leader, than engage in a protracted trade war.

在总统的整个执政期间，他对中国的强硬姿态一直局限于贸易领域。即便在这一领域，他也一再表明，他宁愿与中国领导人习近平达成协议，也不愿陷入一场旷日持久的贸易战。

People close to the president say that in private, he has questioned why the United States would ever go to war over Taiwan.

接近总统的人士表示，他私下里曾质疑美国为何要为了台湾而卷入战争。

To dramatize his disregard for the small island nation, Mr. Trump has been known to hold up a teaspoon to lunch guests at his golf club and say, “This is Taiwan.”

为了戏剧化地表达他对这个小岛国的漠视，特朗普曾向高尔夫俱乐部的午餐客人举起一把茶匙说：“这就是台湾。”
