In a lawsuit on Monday, Attorney General Rob Bonta accuses OpenAI of violating consumer safety law by knowingly developing a "dangerous product without proper oversight," as reported earlier by Reuters.

The lawsuit claims the cyberattack on Hugging Face "was the predictable result of an internal culture at OpenAI that prioritizes growth and rapid model development over safety and accountability."
