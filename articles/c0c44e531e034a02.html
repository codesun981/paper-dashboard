Making the internet better for AI agents might make it worse for everyone else

Until recently, you could split internet users into just two categories: bots and people. But personal AI agents are slowly but surely disrupting that divide by taking on online tasks, from booking flights to reserving hotel rooms. Meta’s Muse alone was downloaded 5 million times in its first month.

The mingling of these agents with human visitors—on an internet designed largely to prioritize people—has gone poorly, with many websites blocking AI agents.

10 Climate Tech Companies to Watch: Moment Energy and its work to repurpose used EV batteries

MIT Technology Review’s Moment Energyis one of10 Climate Tech Companies to Watch 2026, availableexclusively to subscribers.

Demand for energy storage is surging, with capacity expected to grow eightfold in the next decade. To meet that demand, Moment Energy wants to shortcut a supply chain that now flows largely through China by domestically repurposing EV batteries into new grid-connected storage systems.

Ongoing trade battles between the US and China have been a shot in the arm for the company. And soon, it will be able to categorize its repurposed batteries as domestic products in both Canada and the US, where it’s building its next plant.

Is fake grass a bad idea? The AstroTurf wars are far from over.
