Adobe is committing itself to the notion that chatbots and AI agents could be your next design assistant, or even automate creative workflows and repetitive tasks on your behalf. Some of the tools and features announced today at Adobe's Max conference in Miami, Florida, include allowing your preferred third-party AI agents to access and change Creative Cloud projects, new capabilities for Adobe's Firefly AI Assistant, and the ability for AI agents to keep perpetually running recurring work in the background.
