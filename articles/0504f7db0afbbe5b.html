For years, Chinese scientists and their peers from around the world were drawn to the United States, which offered career opportunities at start-ups and ample academic funding. But China has gained significant ground in recent years as an alternative for A.I. researchers.

多年来，中国科学家及其世界各地的同行被美国所吸引，因为美国提供初创企业的职业机会和充足的学术资金。但近年来，中国在成为人工智能研究人员的另一个选择方面已取得重大进展。

As the United States and China vie for A.I. dominance, the Trump administration has made it more difficult for companies to hire foreign researchers, while China has made recruiting the world’s best scientists a national priority.

随着美中两国争夺人工智能主导地位，特朗普政府加大了企业雇用外国研究人员的难度，而中国将招募全球顶尖科学家列为国家优先事项。

Over the past two years, China has flung open its doors to international researchers, introducing a visa for scientists and offering lucrative research grants.

Despite those efforts, foreigners are not coming.

Worse still for China’s ambitions, the number of Chinese scientists working in the United States has risen rather than declined, according to a new study about global A.I. research.

对中国雄心而言更糟的是，一项关于全球人工智能研究的新研究显示，在美国工作的中国科学家数量不降反升。

For every 30 Chinese researchers in the United States working on artificial intelligence in 2025, only one in China made the opposite journey, according to the study, conducted by the Carnegie Endowment for International Peace, a think tank.

该研究由智库卡内基国际和平研究院开展。结果显示，2025年每30名在美从事人工智能研究的中国研究人员中，只有一人反向前往中国。

“Despite U.S.-China tensions, the number of Chinese-origin researchers working in the U.S. actually rose by four percentage points rather than declined” compared with three years ago, said Damien Ma, who led the study.

领导该研究的马旸表示：“尽管美中关系紧张，但原籍中国的在美研究人员数量实际上没有下降，反而上升了四个百分点。”

The study determined the flow of scientists around the world by examining research papers accepted by last year’s NeurIPS, an academic conference focused on machine learning and computational neuroscience. Mr. Ma and his team analyzed where the papers’ authors went to college, received graduate degrees and ended up working.

该研究通过分析去年NeurIPS接收的论文来判断全球科学家的流向。NeurIPS是专注于机器学习和计算神经科学的学术会议。马旸及其团队分析了论文作者就读大学、获得研究生学位以及最终工作的地点。

The results affirm the popular understanding that the United States and China are the two places where most frontier A.I. research is being produced. While China is failing to recruit foreign researchers, the study found, it is seeing a boon in retaining local talent, people who have risen though the domestic education system and are increasingly choosing not to seek work overseas. The United States, on the other hand, maintains its lead by employing foreign researchers, many of whom previously emigrated from China, as well as India, South Korea and elsewhere.

研究结果印证了一个普遍看法：美国和中国产生了大多数前沿人工智能研究。研究发现，中国虽未能招募到外国研究人员，但在留住本土人才方面却大有收获，这些人才通过国内教育体系成长，且越来越倾向于不赴海外工作。另一方面，美国则通过雇用外国研究人员保持领先，其中许多人此前从中国、印度、韩国等地移民而来。

The Carnegie study counts those researchers who migrated from one country to another, but it does not indicate when a researcher moved. One explanation for why the number of Chinese scientists working in America remains high is that they arrived before the Trump administration imposed restrictions on obtaining visas and cut research funding last year.

卡内基的研究统计了从一国迁移至另一国的研究人员，但并未说明迁移时间。在美国工作的中国科学家数量仍居高不下的一个解释是，他们是在特朗普政府去年实施签证限制并削减研究资金之前抵达的。

“There is a delay effect — the researchers in this study mostly made their decisions years ago to study or work here,” said Yingyi Ma, a sociology professor at Syracuse University who studies Asian students in the United States. “The danger for the U.S. is a slow erosion of its appeal to attract talent that compounds over decades. By the time the effect shows up in the data, it is hard to reverse.”

“存在延迟效应——本研究中的研究人员大多在多年前就做出了来美学习或工作的决定，”雪城大学研究美国亚洲学生的社会学教授马颖毅说。“美国的危险在于，其对人才的吸引力正缓慢减弱，这种减弱会在数十年间不断累积。等到数据中显现出效果时，就难以逆转了。”

In 2022, only six of the top 30 institutions publishing papers at NeurIPS were based in China; in 2025, that number rose to 12. Peking University and Tsinghua University, two of China’s top academic institutions, sat at the top of the chart, eclipsing American heavyweights like M.I.T., Stanford and Carnegie Mellon. Chinese researchers also made up 41 percent of the published authors at NeurIPS last year, eclipsing U.S. researchers, who stood at 34 percent.

2022年，在NeurIPS发表论文的前30家机构中，只有六家位于中国；2025年，这一数字升至12家。中国两所顶尖学府北京大学和清华大学位居榜首，超越了麻省理工学院、斯坦福大学和卡内基梅隆大学等重量级美国机构。去年，中国研究人员还占到NeurIPS发表作者的41%，超过美国研究人员的34%。

While China is struggling to recruit foreign talent, it has made some gains in its effort to encourage Chinese researchers and executives abroad to return.

中国在招募外国人才方面困难重重，但在鼓励海外中国研究人员和高管回国方面却取得了一些进展。

The Chinese tech giant Tencent last year appointed Yao Shunyu, a former OpenAI researcher, as its chief A.I. scientist. The move was seen as one of the most notable examples of an A.I. researcher returning to China after having studied and worked in the West.

中国科技巨头腾讯去年任命前OpenAI研究员姚顺雨为其首席人工智能科学家。此举被视为人工智能研究人员在西方学习和工作后回国的最显著例子之一。

Despite China’s new offers of visas and grant money, Silicon Valley still provides more attractive offers and investment opportunities than China for American researchers. Also, moving to a country with few immigrants and a different language is always going to be a challenge.

尽管中国提供了新的签证和经费，但对美国研究人员而言，硅谷仍然给出了比中国更具吸引力的待遇和投资机会。此外，搬到一个移民稀少且语言不同的国家始终是一项挑战。

“For everybody else who’s outside the walled garden, culturally or digitally, it’s still very hard to break into,” Mr. Xu said.

“对于围墙花园之外的其他人来说，无论是文化上还是数字上，仍然很难闯入，”凯文·徐说。

Today, China has an abundance of workplaces for A.I. researchers, including well-funded universities, big tech companies and start-ups, said Kevin Xu, the founder of Interconnected Capital, a hedge fund that invests in A.I. technologies. As Chinese companies like DeepSeek and Moonshot repeatedly make names for themselves on the global stage, they also become more attractive to top talent.

投资人工智能技术的对冲基金互联资本创始人凯文·徐（音）表示，如今中国拥有大量人工智能研究人员的工作场所，包括资金充足的大学、大型科技公司和初创企业。随着DeepSeek和月之暗面等中国公司在全球舞台上屡屡扬名，它们对顶尖人才的吸引力也日益增强。

“The fact that being able to work at DeepSeek gives you just as much professional prestige and future success as working at OpenAI is quite different from what we’ve observed five years ago, when the path was between working at Google versus Tencent,” Mr. Xu said.

“能够在DeepSeek工作所带来的职业声望和未来成功与在OpenAI工作一样，这与我们五年前观察到的情况截然不同，当时人们的选择路径是去谷歌还是去腾讯，”他说。

The ascendancy of Chinese companies might explain why a vast majority of Chinese A.I. researchers choose to remain in the country. But, Mr. Xu added, there’s another reason some researchers remain in China: The government is unwilling to let them go.

中国公司的崛起或许可以解释为何绝大多数中国人工智能研究人员选择留在国内。但凯文·徐补充说，一些研究人员留在中国的另一个原因是：政府不愿放他们走。

This year, the Chinese government restricted the two founders of Manus, an A.I. start-up that was set to be acquired by Meta for $2 billion, from leaving the country. Since then, travel by Chinese A.I. entrepreneurs and researchers — as well as by their spouses and children — has been further curtailed, according to a recent Bloomberg report.
