Singapore’s government said on Thursday the United States had provided little help in its investigation into Alice Huang, the woman at the center of a spy plot that has led to the investigation of Nvidia’s chief executive, Jensen Huang.

新加坡政府周四表示，在其针对黄乐(Alice Huang)展开的调查中，美方几乎未提供协助。黄乐是这起间谍案的核心人物，该案已导致英伟达首席执行官黄仁勋受到调查。

Singaporean authorities held Ms. Huang’s passport to prevent her from leaving the country from June 2025 until February over “potential false declarations” in relation to her company, Megaspeed, which is based in the country, the government said in a statement. She was ultimately released with a “stern warning” and barred from re-entering Singapore.

新加坡政府在声明中说，由于黄乐与其在新加坡注册的公司Megaspeed有关的“潜在虚假申报”，新加坡当局从2025年6月起至今年2月扣留了她的护照，以防止她离境。她最终在收到“严厉警告”后被允许离境，并被禁止再次进入新加坡。

The statement raises new questions about the response of the Trump administration to the case and why the U.S. government did not more forcefully intervene. According to the timeline in the statement, Singaporean authorities had been holding Ms. Huang’s passport well after President Trump was briefed last fall about her relationship with Nvidia’s chief executive and the belief that she was a Chinese intelligence asset.

该声明引发了关于特朗普政府对此案反应的新疑问，以及美国政府为何没有更强有力地介入。根据声明中的时间线，在特朗普总统去年秋天听取有关黄乐与英伟达首席执行官关系、以及她被认为是中国情报人员的简报后，新加坡当局仍扣留着她的护照。

The New York Times has reported that Taiwan began monitoring Ms. Huang in 2024 at the guidance of the U.S. government.

《纽约时报》报道，台湾在美国政府指导下，于2024年开始监视黄乐。

In its statement, Singapore said that it had sought information from an American server maker and close Nvidia business partner, Supermicro, but that the company had provided “limited cooperation.” Supermicro, a firm that packages Nvidia’s A.I. chips onto servers that can be used in data centers, has been at the center of a number of chip smuggling investigations across the globe.

新加坡在声明中说，它曾向美国服务器制造商、英伟达紧密的商业伙伴超微电脑寻求信息，该公司仅提供“有限合作”。超微电脑将英伟达的人工智能芯片组装到可用于数据中心的服务器上，一直处于全球多起芯片走私调查的风口浪尖。

Supermicro was a supplier to Megaspeed, a company incorporated in Singapore and run by Ms. Huang, according to two people with knowledge of the matter.

据两名知情人士透露，超微曾是Megaspeed的供应商。Megaspeed在新加坡注册成立，由黄乐经营。

Singapore said in the statement its authorities raised Supermicro’s “limited cooperation” with the U.S. Department of Justice and Federal Bureau of Investigation in July 2025 and “requested their assistance in securing the company’s cooperation.”

新加坡在声明中说，当局于2025年7月向美国司法部和联邦调查局提出了超微电脑“合作有限”的问题，并“请求他们协助确保该公司合作”。

“Despite these repeated requests, Singapore has to date received limited information or assistance in respect of the company,” the statement said.

“尽管多次提出请求，新加坡迄今在有关该公司的问题上获得的信息或协助有限，”声明说。

Supermicro denied that Megaspeed had ever been a customer. “Supermicro did not sell any products to Megaspeed and has operated in compliance with all applicable U.S. export and re-export control laws,” the company said in a statement.

超微电脑否认Megaspeed曾是它的客户。“超微电脑没有向Megaspeed出售任何产品，并且一直遵守所有适用的美国出口和再出口管制法律，”该公司在一份声明中说。

During questioning, Ms. Huang sought to downplay her role in Megaspeed, according to Singapore’s statement. She was ultimately released because of “insufficient evidence relating to the possible inaccurate declarations for which Megaspeed was being investigated.”

根据新加坡的声明，在接受讯问时，黄乐试图淡化自己在Megaspeed的角色。她最终获释，原因是“关于Megaspeed公司正在接受调查的可能存在不实申报一事证据不足”。

She was issued a “stern warning” and barred from re-entering Singapore. The country’s police are continuing to investigate Megaspeed.

她收到“严厉警告”，并被禁止再次进入新加坡。该国警方仍在继续调查Megaspeed公司。
