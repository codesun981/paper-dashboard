Anthropic has officially gone public. It began trading on the Nasdaq under the ticker symbol "ANT" at $111.50 per share, up from its initial $83 per share price that put company's value at around $1.5 trillion.

The listing comes nearly a month after Anthropic revealed its plans to go public. In its lengthy S-1 filing, the AI company reported recording an $80.7 billion net loss in the first half of this year, most of which comes from an accounting charge that doesn't reflect its business, along with $16.3 billion in revenue.
